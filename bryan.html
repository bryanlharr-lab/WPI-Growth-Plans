BRYANHARR

I believe success means more than growing a business. It means helping people, supporting the community, and leaving things better than you found them.

My background has taken me through college athletics, coaching, officiating, business development, nonprofit work, and community involvement. The common thread is simple: compete hard, serve well, and be useful wherever you are.

More than business.

I work with local business owners, nonprofits, and community organizations with one goal in mind: make a positive difference. Whether that means helping a business grow, supporting a charitable cause, mentoring someone, or simply stepping up where help is needed, I believe in doing the work.

WHAT DRIVES ME

I have always believed that the measure of a person is not just what they build for themselves, but what they contribute to the people around them. I care deeply about local businesses, nonprofits, animals, veterans, and the communities they call home. I support charitable causes involving animals and veterans and look for opportunities to give time, resources, and effort where they can make a real difference.

That mindset carries into the way I work. I like helping people solve problems, connect the dots, and move forward. I would rather be known as someone who showed up, helped, and followed through than someone who simply talked about what should be done.

HOW I SHOW UP

BUSINESS

I help local business owners identify practical opportunities, solve problems, and create stronger customer relationships. I enjoy helping small businesses compete smarter without making things more complicated than they need to be.

COMMUNITY

I work with nonprofits and community organizations and believe in doing whatever I can to make the community better because I am part of it. Sometimes that means time, sometimes connections, sometimes money, and sometimes just being willing to help.

ANIMALS & VETERANS

I care deeply about animal welfare and veterans, and I support charitable causes connected to both. Those are causes I feel personally connected to and want to keep supporting as L3 grows.

MENTORSHIP & SERVICE

Coaching, officiating, mentoring, and serving others have all shaped the way I lead and the way I work with people. Sports taught me accountability, preparation, toughness, and how to deal with people under pressure.

HOME

At home, I share life with my partner, Victoria, along with three dogs and a cat. They are a big part of who I am and a constant reminder that the best parts of life are built around loyalty, trust, humor, and showing up for the people and animals you care about.

I want L3 Growth Advisors to be useful to the businesses and communities it touches. That means helping business owners make stronger decisions, helping organizations connect with resources, and creating enough success to give more back over time.

The goal is not to be the biggest name in the room. The goal is to be the person people trust to show up, tell the truth, do the work, and help move things in a better direction.
